# EMI Schedule Quick Reference Guide

## What's New?

### Fixed EMI Day of Month
All EMIs for a loan are now due on the same day each month (e.g., 5th, 15th, 25th, etc.)

### Complete Repayment Schedule
View the entire loan repayment schedule with detailed breakup of each EMI

## How to Use

### Creating a Loan

1. **Fill loan details** as usual
2. **Select "EMI Day of Month"** (1-31)
   - Choose a day that works best for the customer
   - Example: If salary is on 1st, choose 5th for EMI
3. **Submit** - System generates complete schedule automatically

### Viewing Schedule

1. **Open any loan** from the loans list
2. **Click "Repayment Schedule" tab**
3. **Review the schedule**:
   - See all EMI due dates
   - Check principal and interest breakdown
   - Monitor payment status

## Key Features

### Smart Date Handling
- **31st of month**: Automatically adjusts to last day of shorter months
  - January 31st → February 28th → March 31st
- **Leap years**: Handles February 29th correctly
- **30-day months**: Adjusts automatically

### Visual Status
- 🟢 **Paid**: EMI fully paid
- 🔵 **Pending**: Due but not paid yet
- 🔴 **Overdue**: Past due date
- 🟡 **Partial**: Partially paid

### Schedule Details
Each EMI shows:
- Due date
- Principal amount
- Interest amount
- Total EMI amount
- Opening/closing balance
- Payment status
- Paid date (if paid)

## Examples

### Example 1: Monthly Salary on 1st
- **EMI Day**: 5th of every month
- **Reason**: 4 days after salary for planning

### Example 2: Mid-month Salary
- **EMI Day**: 20th of every month
- **Reason**: Aligns with mid-month salary

### Example 3: Month-end Preference
- **EMI Day**: 30th of every month
- **Reason**: After all monthly expenses

## Tips

💡 **Choose wisely**: Select EMI day based on customer's salary date  
💡 **Avoid month-end**: Days 28-31 may shift in some months  
💡 **Mid-month is safe**: Days 10-20 never shift  
💡 **First week works**: Days 1-7 are consistent
